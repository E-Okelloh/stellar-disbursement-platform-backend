About Us

A people-centered approach to lasting change

We work alongside communities — not for them — to build the capacity, systems, and local leadership needed for durable, self-directed development in some of the most under-resourced corridors of the Horn of Africa.

✓

Integrity

We hold ourselves accountable to the communities we serve and the partners who trust us.

◆

Professionalism

Rigorous, evidence-based programming delivered to the highest operational standard.

◈

Transparency

Open reporting and clear communication with donors, partners, and local leadership.

●

People-Centered

Programs are designed with, not for, the pastoralist communities we partner with.

What We Do

Six thematic areas, one integrated approach

Every program traces back to one of these focus areas, designed to reinforce each other on the ground.

01

Peace & Security

Conflict mitigation and cross-border peacebuilding among pastoralist communities.

02

Governance

Strengthening local institutions, democratic participation, and accountable leadership.

03

Health & Nutrition

Improving access to essential health services and combating malnutrition in remote areas.

04

Humanitarian Assistance

Rapid, dignified response to drought, displacement, and other emergencies.

05

Education & Protection

Expanding access to schooling and safeguarding children and vulnerable groups.

06

WASH

Sustainable water, sanitation, and hygiene infrastructure for underserved communities.

Programs

Major initiatives driving the work forward

Four flagship program areas translate our thematic priorities into action on the ground.

01

Sustainable Livelihoods

Diversifying income sources for pastoralist households through skills-building, market access, and financial inclusion — including digital and mobile-based disbursement channels.

02

Climate Adaptation

Helping communities respond to drought and shifting rainfall patterns through resilient natural resource management and early warning systems.

03

Gender Equity

Expanding women's economic participation and leadership within traditionally patriarchal pastoralist structures.

04

Democratic Governance

Building the civic capacity of local institutions to represent and respond to community needs.

Where We Work

Serving the hardest-to-reach corridors

From the mobile-money ecosystems of Turkana County to the cross-border routes into Uganda, South Sudan, and Ethiopia, our programs meet communities where conventional financial and humanitarian infrastructure often can't.

Turkana County & Kakuma, Kenya

Our home base — a strong mobile-money ecosystem constrained by phone ownership rates and remoteness.

South Sudan Corridor

Cross-border humanitarian and livelihoods support in a conflict-affected region.

Uganda Corridor

Supporting pastoralist communities that move across the Kenya–Uganda border.

Southern Ethiopia

Extending programs to nomadic communities across the northern frontier.

Operating since2006

Nearly two decades of continuous partnership with nomadic pastoralist communities across one of the most challenging operating environments in the region.
